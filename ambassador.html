Life as an ambassador

Meetups, fests and nights out.

You bring people together anyway. As an ambassador, Bloomlet comes along: we help with meetups, college fests and the odd party, and you get to host.

Who can apply

You don’t need a big following.

We look for people others trust, not follower counts.

  • 18 or older, living in India.
  • An active, public Instagram account. Any size.
  • A student, working, or a creator who likes bringing people together.
  • You use Bloomlet yourself, on Android or on the web.
  • Time to share Bloomlet at least twice a month.
  • You follow our Community Guidelines.

Questions

Good to know

Is this a job?
No. It’s a programme you take part in alongside your studies or work, with goodies, a free plan and prizes. There are no fixed hours. Top ambassadors get the chance to work with us.
How many followers do I need?
None in particular. A small account that people trust is worth more to us than a big one.
What happens after I apply?
We read every application and look at your Instagram. If it’s a fit, we’ll call or WhatsApp you on the number you gave us to say hello and send your welcome kit.
Can I stop any time?
Yes. Tell us and you can step away whenever you like.
What can’t ambassadors do?
Pay or offer anything to people for joining, make up claims about Bloomlet, or share anyone’s details. Ambassadors follow our Community Guidelines like everyone else.
