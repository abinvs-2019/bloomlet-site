Why people look for another dating app

Most dating apps are built around speed: a stack of faces, a quick swipe, and a long list of matches who never write. If you’ve felt that on Tinder, Bumble or Hinge, you’re not alone. Bloomlet is built the other way round, for fewer, better conversations.

How Bloomlet works

  • One person at a time. No swiping. You see one profile, with their photos, prompts and interests, and read it before you decide.
  • Say hi with a flower. Your first hello is a flower, and it’s free. Send it about a photo or something they wrote, so the conversation starts with them.
  • Chats that grow. Every chat starts as a bud and blooms as you both keep talking.
  • Real profiles. Everyone signs in with Google or Apple and has a named profile with photos. Nobody is matched with you at random.
  • Free to talk. Start chats with up to 10 new people a day for free. Replies and ongoing chats are never limited.

If you like Hinge’s prompts but want to slow down, or you’re done with swiping on Tinder and Bumble, Bloomlet is worth a try.

Who Bloomlet is for

  • People in India who want to talk before they meet.
  • Anyone who’d rather read a profile than judge a photo in a second.
  • Adults aged 18 and over, with a profile that’s really them.

Bloomlet is free on Google Play and the App Store (on iPhone it’s still listed as SuperSpace for now). Read our dating safety tips before you meet anyone.
