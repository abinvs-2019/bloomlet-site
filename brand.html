Using the brand

Please do, please don’t.

Do
  • Use the files from this page as they are.
  • Leave clear space around the logo, at least the height of the bud.
  • Use the white logo on rose or dark photos, and the ink logo on light ones.
  • Write the name as “Bloomlet”, with a capital B, in text.
Don’t
  • Stretch, rotate, recolour or outline the logo.
  • Add effects like shadows or gradients.
  • Use the bud or the name in your own product’s logo.
  • Suggest Bloomlet endorses you unless we’ve agreed it in writing.
