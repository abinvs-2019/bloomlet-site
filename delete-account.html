Delete your account

Bloomlet (com.superbits.superspace.app) by SuperBits. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

On this page

  1. In the app
  2. By email
  3. What is deleted
  4. What we keep, and for how long
  5. Before you delete

In the app

  1. Open Bloomlet and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Settings, then Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away, and you are signed out.

By email

If you can’t open the app, email info.super.bits@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”, from the email address on your account, or tell us the phone number or Apple or Google account you signed in with. We may ask you to confirm it’s you. We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it’s done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, birthday, photos, prompts, basics and flower.
  • Your messages, and the photos and voice notes you sent in chats.
  • Your blocks, favourites and chat settings.
  • Your Bloom Week flowers and your invite link.
  • The devices registered for notifications.
  • Your sign-in, and the link between your phone number or Apple or Google account and Bloomlet. Signing up again starts a new account.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Purchase, payment and call records, for as long as tax and accounting law requires. They are also the other person’s record of a call or gift they took part in. If you bought something, your name and email stay with those records, to handle refunds and questions about a payment.
  • Bloom Rewards details (legal name, date of birth and PAN), only if a gift card was issued to you, because that is a payment record. Otherwise they are deleted with your account.
  • Reports and safety records, so someone who was removed can’t return by deleting their account.
  • Messages from Fade sessions: an encrypted copy is kept for 90 days after a session ends, only to respond to legal requests, as explained in our Privacy Policy. Deleting your account doesn’t remove it early.
  • Backups: our encrypted backups are kept for up to 30 days, and then deleted.

Before you delete

  • Unused stickers and anything left on a plan are not refunded.
  • If you have earnings to withdraw, or a payout is on its way, the app asks you to finish that first.
  • To stop a subscription renewing, cancel it in Google Play or the App Store. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel it.
